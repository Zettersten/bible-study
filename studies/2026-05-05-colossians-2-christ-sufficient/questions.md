# Colossians 2 — Christ Is Enough - Study Input

*A topical / teaching-outline study walking section-by-section through Colossians 2.*
*Primary translation: **CSB** (Christian Standard Bible).*

## Topic

**Christ is completely sufficient — so don't be led away by human philosophy, legalism, or spiritual shortcuts.**

## Introduction Context

The theme of Colossians 2 is that Christ is completely sufficient, so don't be led away by human philosophy, legalism, or spiritual shortcuts. Paul wants the believers in Colossae to be unified and to have the confidence that they understand God's mysterious plan — and what is that plan? That in Christ himself are all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge. Why is that important? So that they will not be deceived by well-crafted arguments and false doctrine. Is that still true today?

## Full Passage — Colossians 2 (CSB)

### Paul's Concern (vv. 1–5)

> **¹** For I want you to know how greatly I am struggling for you, for those in Laodicea, and for all who have not seen me in person.
>
> **²** I want their hearts to be encouraged and joined together in love, so that they may have all the riches of complete understanding and have the knowledge of God's mystery — Christ.
>
> **³** In him are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge.
>
> **⁴** I am saying this so that no one will deceive you with arguments that sound reasonable.
>
> **⁵** For I may be absent in body, but I am with you in spirit, rejoicing to see how well ordered you are and the strength of your faith in Christ.

**READ Col 2:2–3**

> **Q1.** Why is God's plan mysterious?
>
> **Q2.** What does it mean that all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are in Christ?

---

### Walking in Christ (vv. 6–7)

> **⁶** So then, just as you have received Christ Jesus as Lord, continue to walk in him,
>
> **⁷** being rooted and built up in him and established in the faith, just as you were taught, and overflowing with gratitude.

Now that we have accepted Christ as our Lord… what comes next? Obedience. We must follow him. How do we do that?

**READ Col 2:6–7**

> **Q3.** How do we implement the ideas in verse seven?

---

### Warning Against False Philosophy (v. 8)

> **⁸** Be careful that no one takes you captive through philosophy and empty deceit based on human tradition, based on the elements of the world, rather than Christ.

When we conceptualize these things, there is a danger we face.

**READ v. 8**

> **Q4.** Can you give examples of empty philosophy and high-sounding nonsense that come from human thinking and demonic powers?

### United with Christ — Death, Burial, Resurrection (vv. 9–15)

> **⁹** For the entire fullness of God's nature dwells bodily in Christ,
>
> **¹⁰** and you have been filled by him, who is the head over every ruler and authority.
>
> **¹¹** You were also circumcised in him with a circumcision not done with hands, by putting off the body of flesh, in the circumcision of Christ,
>
> **¹²** when you were buried with him in baptism, in which you were also raised with him through faith in the working of God, who raised him from the dead.
>
> **¹³** And when you were dead in trespasses and in the uncircumcision of your flesh, he made you alive with him and forgave us all our trespasses.
>
> **¹⁴** He erased the certificate of debt, with its obligations, that was against us and opposed to us, and has taken it away by nailing it to the cross.
>
> **¹⁵** He disarmed the rulers and authorities and disgraced them publicly; he triumphed over them in him.

Paul says that when we were saved, our sin nature was "cut away." Does that mean we no longer have a sin nature?

**READ Col 2:11–15**

> **Q5.** What does it mean that our sin nature was circumcised?

---

### Freedom from Legalism and Asceticism (vv. 16–23)

> **¹⁶** Therefore, don't let anyone judge you in regard to food and drink or in the matter of a festival or a new moon or a Sabbath day.
>
> **¹⁷** These are a shadow of what was to come; the substance is Christ.
>
> **¹⁸** Let no one condemn you by delighting in ascetic practices and the worship of angels, claiming access to a visionary realm. Such people are inflated by empty notions of their unspiritual mind.
>
> **¹⁹** He doesn't hold on to the head, from whom the whole body, nourished and held together by its ligaments and tendons, grows with growth from God.
>
> **²⁰** If you died with Christ to the elements of this world, why do you live as if you still belonged to the world? Why do you submit to regulations:
>
> **²¹** "Don't handle, don't taste, don't touch"?
>
> **²²** All these regulations refer to what is destined to perish by being used up; they are human commands and doctrines.
>
> **²³** Although these have a reputation for wisdom by promoting self-made religion, false humility, and severe treatment of the body, they are not of any value in curbing self-indulgence.

Paul then says that we are no longer required to observe dietary rules or traditional Jewish holidays or celebrations.

**READ Col 2:16–23**

> **Q6.** If we are no longer required to observe all these customs, rules, and regulations, how are we to respond to our new faith?

---

## Simple Summary

In Christ, every treasure of wisdom is already yours — so walk rooted, refuse counterfeits, and let the gospel (not rule-keeping) reshape your daily life.
