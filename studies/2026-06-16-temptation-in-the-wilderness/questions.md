# Jesus and Me: Tempted in the Wilderness

**Date:** June 16, 2026  
**Passage:** Matthew 4:1-10  
**Type:** Teaching Outline Study

## Original Class Outline

In the account of the temptation of Jesus in the wilderness……… Satan was not only tempting Jesus to sin………he was tempting him to abandon his trust in the Father as well as compromising the standards that God had established……..the same temptations confront you and me today……So let's take a look at this passage…………

---

**READ:** Matthew 4:1–10

**Q. What are some of the ways that we put God to the test ("tempt") the Lord by our actions?** (See Deuteronomy 6:16 — "You shall not tempt…..")

- Taking foolish risks and expecting God to bail us out
  - Faith obeys God – presumption expects God to rescue us from our own recklessness

- Demanding signs before obeying
  - If the bank approves a loan, it must be God's will

- Living in deliberate sin, while expecting God's protection
  - The Israelites often wanting God's protection while refusing his ways

- Treating prayer like a challenge to God
  - These statements are different from honest doubts

- Presuming upon God's grace

---

**Q. What does faith look like instead?**

---

## Let's take a deeper dive into these three Temptations

### "COMMAND THESE STONES TO BECOME BREAD………." (4:3)
- Use your power (own abilities) independently of the Father to meet your needs
  - ex. Sacrificing family time or worship for financial gain
- Compromising integrity to get ahead
- Letting the "easy way" determine your obedience

### "THROW YOURSELF DOWN………." (4:5–7)
- Expecting God to rescue us from foolish decisions

### "ALL THESE KINGDOMS I WILL GIVE YOU…….." (4:8-9)
- You can get what you want without doing it God's way
  - ex. Success without integrity -- wealth without honesty – influence without humility -- pleasure without obedience
