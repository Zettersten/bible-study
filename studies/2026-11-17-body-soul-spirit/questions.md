# Body, Soul, and Spirit Sanctification — Study Input

**Date:** November 17, 2026\
**Translation:** CSB (Christian Standard Bible)\
**Format:** Topical / Teaching Outline, following the class reading order

## Topic

Man’s triune nature (body, soul, and spirit), what it means that we are made “in our likeness,” the difference among souls, whether unbelievers have a spirit, the incentive to walk in holiness, and how we feed and train our soul and spirit so that we are blameless at the coming of the Lord.

## Class Introduction

The Bible tells us that we are triune beings, possessing a body, soul, and spirit, and that we should keep these three parts of ourselves blameless at the coming of the Lord. When Jesus comes again for his bride, he wants to find us walking in holiness and ready for his return.

Holiness is not only about what we do with our bodies. It is also about what we feed our souls and spirits. God wants us to be blameless at his coming, and blameless does not mean perfect.

## The Soul

The Greek word for soul is *psychē*, and we use it to mean the seat of emotion, reason, thought, will, and intellect. The soul is our self-consciousness.

**Matthew 22:37** (CSB)

> **37** He said to him, “Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.”

> **Q4.** Are all souls alike?
>
> **Q5.** What guidance do we have for dealing with different types of souls? Eph 4:2

**Ephesians 4:1-3** (CSB)

> **1** Therefore I, the prisoner in the Lord, urge you to walk worthy of the calling you have received,
> **2** with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love,
> **3** making every effort to keep the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace.

## The Spirit

The spirit is the essential nature that gives life to our being. The spirit is our deeper self, the part with a God-consciousness and with the ability to commune with him.

**1 Corinthians 2:11-15** (CSB)

> **11** For who knows a person’s thoughts except his spirit within him? In the same way, no one knows the thoughts of God except the Spirit of God.
> **12** Now we have not received the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who comes from God, so that we may understand what has been freely given to us by God.
> **13** We also speak these things, not in words taught by human wisdom, but in those taught by the Spirit, explaining spiritual things to spiritual people.
> **14** But the person without the Spirit does not receive what comes from God’s Spirit, because it is foolishness to him; he is not able to understand it since it is evaluated spiritually.
> **15** The spiritual person, however, can evaluate everything, and yet he himself cannot be evaluated by anyone.

> **Q6.** Do unbelievers have a spirit? 1 Cor 2:14
>
> **Q7.** What incentive do we have to walk in holiness? 1 Thess 5:23

## Blameless, Not Perfect

We must be mindful not only of what we do with our body, but also of what we feed our soul and spirit, because God wants us to be blameless at his coming. Blameless does not mean perfection.

> **Q8.** Can we sin in our soul and spirit?

---

## Walk in the Spirit

God calls us to exercise our spirit so that it is stronger than our body. Scripture says to “walk in the Spirit and you shall not fulfill the lust of the flesh.”

**Galatians 5:16-17** (CSB)

> **16** I say, then, walk by the Spirit and you will certainly not carry out the desire of the flesh.
> **17** For the flesh desires what is against the Spirit, and the Spirit desires what is against the flesh; these are opposed to each other, so that you don’t do what you want.

**1 Timothy 4:6-10** (CSB)

> **6** If you point these things out to the brothers and sisters, you will be a good servant of Christ Jesus, nourished by the words of the faith and the good teaching that you have followed.
> **7** But have nothing to do with pointless and silly myths. Rather, train yourself in godliness.
> **8** For the training of the body has limited benefit, but godliness is beneficial in every way, since it holds promise for the present life and also for the life to come.
> **9** This saying is trustworthy and deserves full acceptance.
> **10** For this reason we labor and strive, because we have put our hope in the living God, who is the Savior of all people, especially of those who believe.

> **Q9.** How does feeding your soul affect the desires of your flesh? 1 Tim 4:8
>
> **Q10.** Can you name some of the ways that you feed and train your spirit?

## Ready for the Coming of the Lord

Paul reminds us that Jesus is coming again. His coming will not be a surprise for us, but it will be for non-believers. 1 Thess 5:4

We may not know the day of Jesus’ coming, but we can prepare for it by being sober-minded, vigilant, and walking in the light.

**1 Thessalonians 5:4-10** (CSB)

> **4** But you, brothers and sisters, are not in the dark, for this day to surprise you like a thief.
> **5** For you are all children of light and children of the day. We do not belong to the night or the darkness.
> **6** So then, let us not sleep, like the rest, but let us stay awake and be self-controlled.
> **7** For those who sleep, sleep at night, and those who get drunk, get drunk at night.
> **8** But since we belong to the day, let us be self-controlled and put on the armor of faith and love, and a helmet of the hope of salvation.
> **9** For God did not appoint us to wrath, but to obtain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,
> **10** who died for us, so that whether we are awake or asleep, we may live together with him.
