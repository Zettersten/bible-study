# 1 Thessalonians 4 — Study Input

**Date:** October 20, 2026\
**Translation:** CSB (Christian Standard Bible)\
**Format:** Topical / Teaching Outline, following the class reading order

## Topic

Living to please God, the will of God, loving believers, a quiet life of work, the coming of the Lord, and the days of Noah and Lot.

## Class Introduction

Gary (Pastor Gary Hamrick of Cornerstone Chapel in Leesburg, Virginia) says there are three areas of our faith that many Christians do not fully understand: Israel and the Jews, spiritual gifts, and the Second Coming of Christ. The class would add one more: how can I know God’s will?

Paul begins chapter 4 by reminding the church at Thessalonica to keep living in a way that pleases God. That means living according to God’s will.

*The class introduction is preserved here in clean markdown. The accompanying study works through each question in order. Questions retain their original wording, with punctuation normalized and numbers added. The unnumbered reflection remains unnumbered.*

> **Reflection:** How willing are you to live according to the will of God?

## God’s Will for Your Life

**READ 1 Thessalonians 4:1-8**

**1 Thessalonians 4:1-8** (CSB)

> **1** Additionally then, brothers and sisters, we ask and encourage you in the Lord Jesus, that as you have received instruction from us on how you should live and please God—as you are doing—do this even more.
> **2** For you know what commands we gave you through the Lord Jesus.
> **3** For this is God’s will, your sanctification: that you keep away from sexual immorality,
> **4** that each of you knows how to control his own body in holiness and honor,
> **5** not with lustful passions, like the Gentiles, who don’t know God.
> **6** This means one must not transgress against and take advantage of a brother or sister in this manner, because the Lord is an avenger of all these offenses, as we also previously told and warned you.
> **7** For God has not called us to impurity but to live in holiness.
> **8** Consequently, anyone who rejects this does not reject man, but God, who gives you his Holy Spirit.

## The Days of Noah and Lot

**READ Luke 17:26-35**

**Luke 17:26-37** (CSB)

> **26** “Just as it was in the days of Noah, so it will be in the days of the Son of Man:
> **27** People went on eating, drinking, marrying and being given in marriage until the day Noah boarded the ark, and the flood came and destroyed them all.
> **28** It will be the same as it was in the days of Lot: People went on eating, drinking, buying, selling, planting, building.
> **29** But on the day Lot left Sodom, fire and sulfur rained from heaven and destroyed them all.
> **30** It will be like that on the day the Son of Man is revealed.
> **31** On that day, a man on the housetop, whose belongings are in the house, must not come down to get them. Likewise the man who is in the field must not turn back.
> **32** Remember Lot’s wife!
> **33** Whoever tries to make his life secure will lose it, and whoever loses his life will preserve it.
> **34** I tell you, on that night two will be in one bed; one will be taken and the other will be left.
> **35** Two women will be grinding grain together; one will be taken and the other left.”
> **37** “Where, Lord?” they asked him. He said to them, “Where the corpse is, there also the vultures will be gathered.”

*Verse 36 does not appear in the CSB text. The CSB footnote notes that some manuscripts include it: “Two will be in a field: One will be taken, and the other will be left.”*

**Class points from the outline:**

- The timing of glorified bodies
- The appearance of Jesus (the Second Coming)
- Dead / Alive
